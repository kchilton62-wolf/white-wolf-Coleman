Purpose & Passion

Hello, my name is Karen Coleman and I am the go-to person for tips on youth leadership and communication skill building. I started out as a young girl with a learning disability and reading disorder who hated being the subject of repeated failure in the school system. With the right mentoring and emotional health instructions, I learned resilience and courage are my superpowers. Now, I am a retired public librarian, distinguished Toastmaster, published author, and entrepreneur of a nonprofit teen leadership business. When I'm not working with youth to grow their potential, I continue my learning journey in college and independent studies of the skills I long to master.
